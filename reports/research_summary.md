# Research Summary

## Purpose

This project examines how residents, local businesses and local authorities prioritise recovery needs after a crisis affecting the built environment.

## Approach

A simulated dataset of 240 anonymised responses was grouped by stakeholder type and recovery category. Average priority scores were calculated and compared using a grouped bar chart and normalised heatmap.

## Findings

- Residents prioritised housing most strongly.
- Local businesses prioritised infrastructure most strongly.
- Local authorities also prioritised infrastructure most strongly.
- The results suggest that recovery planning may need to balance housing-led community priorities with infrastructure-led business and authority priorities.

## Limitations

The data are simulated and illustrative. They cannot be used to make claims about real communities, organisations or recovery programmes.

## Future work

- Replace simulated data with an openly licensed real dataset
- Add statistical significance testing
- Add qualitative theme analysis
- Develop an interactive public-facing dashboard
